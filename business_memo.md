# Business Memo

## Subject: Customer Churn Risk Assessment

The exploratory analysis identified several behavioral patterns strongly associated with customer churn risk.

Customers with lower purchase frequency and declining session activity demonstrated noticeably higher churn probability. Reduced engagement appears to be one of the strongest early warning indicators.

Support-ticket-heavy customers also showed elevated churn behavior, suggesting that unresolved service dissatisfaction may contribute to customer loss.

Return and refund activity may indicate product dissatisfaction or declining customer trust in the platform experience.

The analysis also suggests that highly engaged customers with consistent purchasing behavior tend to remain retained for longer periods.

Before launching any retention campaign, the company should further investigate:

- declining customer activity trends,
- support resolution effectiveness,
- refund and return behavior,
- campaign engagement quality,
- customer inactivity periods.

Targeted intervention strategies focused on inactive and support-sensitive customers may help reduce future churn risk.
